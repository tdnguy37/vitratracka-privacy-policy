3. How We Use Your Data

We use the collected data for the following essential purposes:

  • Core Service Delivery: To enable you to log and track your vitamin and supplement intake and display your history.
  • Account Management: To manage and authenticate your user account, and to securely maintain your profile.
  • Security: To detect and prevent technical issues and fraud.
  • Internal Research: We may use aggregated, anonymized data (data that cannot be linked back to you) for internal analysis to improve the App's functionality and user experience.

Commitment to Health Data Privacy (Crucial Apple Disclosure): We will never use or disclose your Health and Fitness Data (vitamins, dosages, nutrition) to third parties for advertising, marketing, or use-based data mining purposes. Your data is used exclusively to provide and improve the VitaTracka service to you.

5. Your Data Protection Rights

You have the right to control and request information about your data.

A. Right to Data Deletion (Account Closure)

You have the right to request the deletion of your account and all associated personal and health data.

  • Request Method: You can initiate the account deletion process by using the **"Delete Account" button/setting** located within the App's user profile or settings menu.
  • Deletion Timeframe: Upon confirmation of your deletion request, **we will delete all personal and health data immediately** from our active databases and servers.

B. Right to Access and Correction

You may request access to, or correction of, your personal data by contacting us at the email address provided above.

6. Children's Privacy

VitaTracka is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected Personal Data from a child under 13 without verification of parental consent, we will take steps to remove that information from our servers.
